Welcome

Three 90-minute sessions on how operational intelligence platforms such as Palantir's work, on synthetic data: what an analyst sees, what an engineer builds underneath, and who watches either of them.

Session 1 · Start here

Investigate

Log in as a patrol officer and notice what is missing; switch to the financial-crime analyst. Trace the risk-tagged operator and hit a Checkpoint. Then try Oversight: Oversight opens for the data protection officer or auditor; as analyst you'll be refused, and that refusal is logged.

Session 2

Build and govern

Open the pipeline and find where the ontology was made. Trace the HEALTH marking from the clinic data downstream, then try to remove it: first as the analyst, then as the data protection officer, who can also restore it.

Lower the entity-resolution threshold until two Omar Rahmans become one, read the signals that merged them, and unmerge them. Turn on Karlsruhe mode and see which joins the purpose rules forbid.

Session 3

Agent, leads, oversight

Ask the simulated AIP agent the Session 1 question and read its trace: every tool call, what was denied, and which object backs each sentence. Approve or reject its staged edits, then catch the link it invented.

Open the Leads tab and read the address-confidence formula: watch the score drop when the health record is withheld. Close in Oversight with your own trail, then ask where all of this actually lives.

Apps: Investigate (Agent and Leads tabs), then Oversight · Cases: ICE ELITE, NHS Federated Data Platform, Maven; POL-INTEL and European exits
