# Issues found: Lena (law)

Each issue gives the step or page, what the runthrough, card or tool says, what I found, and the screenshot (all in `shots/`).

1. **2.3 Markings.** The DPO's "Remove marking" on HEALTH leaves `clinic_registrations.csv` with no marking at all. Its rows become visible to all five roles, including the Platform contractor, who can read patient names, dates of birth and addresses. The recorded legal basis stays "Art. 9(2)(h)", and the only purpose offered is "Oversight". Screenshots: `23e_dpo_removed.png`, `23h_contractor_sees_clinic_rows.png`.
2. **2.3, instructor box.** "Ask students to undo this before 2.4." There is no undo or restore control after removal, and the runthrough says Reset dataset keeps marking removals. Screenshot: `23e_dpo_removed.png`.
3. **The DPO role (legal).** The DPO is described as "read-only" yet is the only role that may remove a marking. Under Articles 38(6) and 39 GDPR the DPO advises and monitors. Making the DPO the person who lifts protections creates a conflict of interest the tool doesn't flag. Screenshot: `22b_pings_grey.png` (role description).
4. **Withheld markers reveal health status.** The analyst sees "clinic registrations — HEALTH withheld" in Leads and "Home address withheld — marking HEALTH" in the inspector, and Lineage lists `norm_clinic_registrations row 9` for Omar Rahman. Under Article 9 GDPR, being in clinic data is itself health data. Screenshots: `34c_leads_analyst.png`, `12f_clarasilva.png`, `21g_lineage_omar.png`.
5. **Withheld markers reveal telecom data.** As analyst (no TELECOM), the inspector lists ping record IDs for Kareem Haddad. C. Silva's note reveals the names observed in the pings. Screenshots: `12c_haddad_inspector.png`, `12e_csilva.png`.

13. **Karlsruhe mode framing (legal).** R2 is closer to Article 9 GDPR and Article 10 LED (and the court's "hypothetical re-collection" test for changes of purpose) than to the 2023 judgment, which concerned data the police already held. The tool also never says that the court addressed the legislature. Screenshot: `25b_R2_details.png`.
